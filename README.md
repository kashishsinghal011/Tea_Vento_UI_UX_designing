# 🍵 Vento — Cinematic Tea Experience

> A premium, immersive tea website designed around cinematic scrolling, interactive product motion, and the visual storytelling of Vento Tea.

Vento is an interactive tea brand experience built to transform a traditional tea product website into an immersive digital journey.

Instead of presenting products as static cards, the website uses scroll-driven animation, product movement, parallax, typography, atmospheric effects, and storytelling to make the tea products feel like physical objects moving through the experience.

---

## ✨ Overview

Vento tells the story of tea through a continuous visual journey:

**Origin → Blend → Brew → Ritual → Product → Josh**

The website combines real Vento tea packaging with a cinematic animation system inspired by premium interactive product experiences.

The focus is not only on selling tea, but on communicating:

- Indian tea culture
- warmth
- energy
- craftsmanship
- tradition
- premium product presentation
- the ritual of brewing tea

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

The main goals of the project are to:

- Create a premium digital identity for Vento Tea
- Showcase real Vento tea products interactively
- Build a cinematic scrolling experience
- Use product photography as the main visual element
- Create meaningful scroll-driven animations
- Build a strong storytelling experience
- Maintain smooth and performant interactions
- Provide a responsive experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🎬 Cinematic Hero

The hero section introduces Vento through a large-scale product experience.

Features include:

- Scroll-driven product movement
- Product scaling and rotation
- Multi-layer parallax
- Animated typography
- Atmospheric lighting
- Floating botanical elements
- Steam effects
- Scroll indicator

The product is treated as the protagonist rather than a static image.
